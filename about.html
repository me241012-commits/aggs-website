About Auckland Girls' Grammar School

Empowering Young Women Through Education And Leadership

Who We Are

Auckland Girls' Grammar School is one of New Zealand's oldest and most respected secondary schools for young women. We are committed to academic excellence, cultural diversity, and empowering students to become confident leaders in their communities.

English

Develop strong communication, critical thinking, and literacy skills.

Science

Explore biology, chemistry, physics, and real world scientific inquiry.

Mathematics

Build problem solving skills through algebra, geometry, and statistics.

Our Mission

We aim to inspire young women to reach their full potential through a supportive learning environment, strong academic foundations, and opportunities for leadership and personal growth.
